About

A deliberate practice built on rigorous preparation.

Ankit Mukherjee has practised corporate and commercial law before the Calcutta High Court for over twelve years, appearing on behalf of listed companies, private equity funds, and high-net-worth individuals in matters ranging from multi-crore contractual disputes to cross-border enforcement proceedings.

Prior to establishing independent chambers, he trained under Senior Advocate R.K. Mitra — one of the Calcutta Bar’s most respected commercial lawyers — and spent three years advising the litigation desk of a leading national firm. That combination of courtroom experience and transactional grounding informs how he approaches every brief.

He holds a B.A. LLB (Hons.) from the National Law School of India University, Bangalore, graduating in the top decile of his cohort, and a Postgraduate Diploma in International Commercial Arbitration from NALSAR University of Law, Hyderabad.

“The client’s problem is a legal problem second. First, it is a practical one — and that is where I begin.”

Practice Areas

Where I appear and advise.

  • 01

    Corporate Disputes & Commercial Litigation

    Contractual claims, shareholder oppression, winding-up petitions, and enforcement of foreign judgments before the Calcutta High Court (Original Side) and the NCLT, Kolkata Bench.

  • 02

    International & Domestic Arbitration

    Counsel and arbitration advisory under LCIA, ICC, and the Arbitration & Conciliation Act 1996. Appeared in institutional arbitrations seated in Singapore, London, and Mumbai.

  • 03

    M&A Disputes & Post-Acquisition Litigation

    Indemnity claims, warranty & representation disputes, earn-out disagreements, and escrow-related litigation arising from merger and acquisition transactions across sectors.

  • 04

    Regulatory Matters & White-Collar Defence

    SEBI enforcement and adjudication proceedings, ED investigations, CCI compliance matters, and internal investigations for listed and unlisted companies facing regulatory scrutiny.

  • 05

    Insolvency & Restructuring

    Representation of financial creditors, operational creditors, and resolution applicants in CIRP and liquidation proceedings before the NCLT and NCLAT under the Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code 2016.
